# Lịch 10 tuần, 20 buổi / 10-Week, 20-Session Schedule

[← Tổng quan](INDEX.md) · [Đồ án](projects/final_project.md)

| Tuần | Buổi 1 | Buổi 2 | Mốc nộp |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Training lifecycle và stage gates | FLOPs/VRAM/storage/time budget | Training charter |
| 2 | Provenance, consent, license | Data lineage, access, retention, risk review | Governance pack |
| 3 | Synthetic generation strategy | Filtering, dedup, contamination, QA | Dataset v1 |
| 4 | Tokenizer audit | Base model architecture/license/precision decision | Model decision record |
| 5 | CPT objective và data mixture | Reduced CPT smoke test, forgetting checks | CPT report |
| 6 | Instruction taxonomy/SFT | Curriculum, packing, loss masking, SFT run | SFT checkpoint |
| 7 | DDP/FSDP/ZeRO concepts | Observability, checkpoint/resume/failure drill | Ops runbook |
| 8 | Preference data/DPO | Reward/eval alignment, regression audit | Aligned checkpoint |
| 9 | Capability/safety evaluation | Red team, contamination, release gates | Eval dossier |
| 10 | Merge/quantize/package/model card | Serving benchmark, optional DeepSpec, submission | Release candidate |

Mỗi buổi 90–120 phút; lab quy mô nhỏ được ưu tiên hơn job lớn thiếu kiểm soát.
